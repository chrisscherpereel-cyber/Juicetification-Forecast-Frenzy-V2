How every module works

  • 1
    Read the objective & the formula — what you're learning and the math behind it.
  • 2
    Apply it — your choice — compute it yourself, estimate then reveal, or show the answer.
  • 3
    Write the Excel formula — the app runs it on the grid and checks your answer.
  • 4
    Read the feedback — is your answer reasonable, and what's even better?
  • 5
    Answer the guiding question & try again — then a green ✅ points you to the next tab.
🏪 Running Juicetification: pick your forecast → calculate the staffing & prep plan yourself → set the sliders and open for the day. You'll see the profit, customer satisfaction, and where over- or under-forecasting cost you money.
📊 In Excel, always use cell references. Point to the cells (=AVERAGE(D5:F5)), never type the numbers (=(280+300+315)/3 is rejected). That's the skill employers want.

🎓 The debrief matters most. It shows your biggest forecast miss and what it cost — then asks for one rule you'll keep. That rule is the real takeaway.
🎲 Your data is unique. Everyone gets a different Session ID and scenario, so compare methods — not answers.
💡 Stuck on a number? Pick "Estimate, then reveal" to see the worked solution, then try the Excel version yourself.
⚖️ Remember the trade-off. Under-forecasting loses sales & goodwill; over-forecasting spoils fruit. They don't cost the same.
